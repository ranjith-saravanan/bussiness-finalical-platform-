# MyBIZ User Guide

## Getting Started

1. **Sign Up / Login**:
   - Access `http://localhost:5173/register` to create an account.
   - A default business venture is automatically established for you.

2. **Personal Finance Flow**:
   - Navigate to **Income** (`/income`) to log your primary earnings and recurring cashflow.
   - Navigate to **Expenses** (`/expenses`) to record bills, housing, and discretionary spending.
   - Navigate to **Budgets** (`/budgets`) to set monthly spending caps by category and track real-time utilization.
   - Navigate to **Savings** (`/savings`) to set targets (e.g. Emergency Fund) and update balances.
   - Navigate to **Cash Flow** (`/cashflow`) to inspect your monthly net liquidity progression.

3. **Business Operations Flow**:
   - Use the top navigation bar to switch between multiple business entities or create new ones in **Business Hub** (`/business`).
   - Register clients in **Customers** (`/customers`).
   - Add goods or billable hourly rates in **Products & Services** (`/products`).
   - Issue invoices and record customer payments in **Sales** (`/sales`).
   - Track OpEx overhead in **Business Hub** (`/business`).

4. **Intelligence & Advisory**:
   - Open **AI Advisor** (`/ai-advisor`) to chat with your copilot.
   - Ask:
     - *"How much did I spend this month?"*
     - *"Where am I spending the most?"*
     - *"Can I afford this expense?"*
     - *"How is my business performing?"*
     - *"Give me a simple savings recommendation."*
   - Review personalized high/medium/low strategic advice cards.
   - Inspect projections on the **Forecasting** page (`/forecast`).

5. **Exports & Alerts**:
   - Download CSV reports on the **Export** page (`/export`).
   - Review budget limits and milestone triggers in **Notifications** (`/notifications`).
